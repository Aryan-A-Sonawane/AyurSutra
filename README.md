
 # 🌿 AyurSutra – Panchakarma & Hospital Management Platform

> A **comprehensive AI-powered Panchakarma & Ayurveda Practice Management Software** designed for **SIH’25**, integrating **hospital administration, patient engagement, practitioner workflows, and modern digital healthcare solutions** with a traditional Ayurvedic touch.

---

## 🚀 Problem Statement

The current Ayurvedic and Panchakarma healthcare ecosystem faces challenges in:
- **Fragmented workflows**: Reports, prescriptions, and follow-ups are scattered.
- **Limited patient engagement**: No centralized reminders, routines, or therapy tracking.
- **Hospital inefficiencies**: Lack of digital platforms for hospital admin and staff coordination.
- **Accessibility barriers**: Difficulty for patients to find hospitals, choose doctors, or access online consultations.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

**AyurSutra** provides a **digital-first Ayurveda healthcare ecosystem** with:
- **Multi-role authentication** (Patients, Practitioners, Hospitals, Receptionists, Admins)
- **End-to-end practitioner workflow** (Reports → Scheduling → Reminders → Prescriptions)
- **Hospital ecosystem integration** (Hospital profiles, services, reviews, staff management)
- **Patient-centered features** (Routine checklists, therapy tracking, guardian alerts, chatbot)
- **Video consultations with payment integration**
- **Intelligent chatbot** for appointment scheduling, consultation requests, and hospital discovery.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🧑‍⚕️ Practitioner Side
- Comprehensive **Add Report Form** with therapy type, progress score, prescriptions, and care guidelines.
- **Image upload** for session reports and prescriptions.
- **Daily routine creation** with customizable reminders → displayed as **interactive patient checklists**.
- **Multi-step reminder system** (time-based notifications after therapy sessions).
- **Practitioner Dashboard** with:
  - Report creation & viewing  
  - Routine assignment  
  - Calendar view with appointment color codes  

---

### 🏥 Hospital Management
- **Hospital Registration System**:
  - Owner details, social links, services, banner, timings, Google Maps location
  - Customer reviews & feedback  
- **Admin Panel**:
  - Invite and verify doctors via email  
  - Role-based access (Doctor, Receptionist, Admin)  
  - Edit hospital banner, services, timings  
  - Calendar view for all appointments  

---

### 👨‍👩‍👧 Patient Experience
- **Enhanced registration** with guardian contact → notifications sent to both patient & guardian.
- **Therapy tracking card** with doctor & hospital details, prescriptions, and routines.
- **Hospital search by pincode**, with **filters** for doctor gender preference.
- **Video Consultation Booking** with fee payment and confirmation flow.
- **Daily routine checklist** visible in dashboard with interactive completion.
- **Reports & Prescriptions** stored date-wise and retrievable anytime.
- **Notification Bell Icon** for reminders, scheduling updates, and alerts.
- **Rule-based AI Chatbot**:
  - Book appointments  
  - Find hospitals  
  - Request consultations  
  - View reports  

---

### 📚 Educational Blogs
- **Doctor Blog Post Creation**: Add blogs with text, images, and YouTube video links.
- **Hospital Blog Tab**: Hospital-specific blogs visible on hospital profile page.
- **Patient Blog Reader UI**: Responsive, modern blog reading experience.

---

### 📅 Calendar Integration
- **Color-coded appointment calendar** for Patients, Practitioners, and Hospital Admin.
- **Real-time updates** when appointments are booked or rescheduled.
- **Drag & Drop scheduling** for doctors → patients get notified automatically.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: React + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS + shadcn/ui  
- **Backend**: Supabase (Auth, Database, Storage, Real-time Notifications)  
- **Video Consultation**: WebRTC Integration  
- **Payments**: Razorpay / Stripe (configurable)  
- **Chatbot**: Rule-based decision tree with future LLM integration  
- **Calendar**: Custom React Calendar with appointment mapping  

---

## 📊 Impact

- 🌍 **Accessibility**: Patients can easily find nearby hospitals and book consultations.  
- 🏥 **Efficiency**: Hospitals digitize services, schedules, and patient engagement.  
- 👨‍⚕️ **Practitioner empowerment**: Doctors can focus on treatment while system handles reports, reminders, and patient tracking.
